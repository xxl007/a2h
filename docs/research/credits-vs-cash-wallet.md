# Cash wallet beats credits for A2H

> Research study, 21 October 2026, prepared by the PM agent for the product owner and the design partner as input to ADR 0002 (money model). Sourced research notes: [notes/credits-vs-cash-wallet/](notes/credits-vs-cash-wallet/). Claims marked "to be verified" in the text are flagged in the notes' Gaps sections.

A2H should keep the planned cash wallet and not replace it with a credit system; the case evidence, the incentive experiments and EU/French payments law all point the same way. Participant credits that convert to cash are at best deferred cash under a new name, which adds no incentive the Stripe balance does not already provide, and at worst a points programme whose defining complaints are thresholds, expiry and confiscated balances. A platform-issued convertible credit is electronic money under EMD2/PSD2 as applied in France: it brings ACPR authorisation, an unwaivable right for participants to redeem at par for free, a full anti-money-laundering programme, financial-liability accounting and a Stripe "restricted business" category, with no VAT or DAC7 relief in return. Closed-loop credit economies with asymmetric populations (earners on one side, spenders on the other, which is exactly A2H's expert/principal split) hoarded, died for lack of demand, were farmed by bots or were resented as a tax; Japan's largest time bank redeemed **0.6% of banked credits in 2010**, and Reddit's non-refundable coins triggered the loudest backlash in the corpus. Behaviourally, **cash beat gift cards 79% to 68%** on response rate in a 2026 randomised panel experiment, and no expert network found pays anything but cash. The levers with evidence behind them for signup and participation are cash itself, a payout threshold of at most about €10, a published cadence, an immediate-plus-conditional pay structure, a deadline that voids the reward, and identity verification gating the first payout, all of which A2H's reserve-plus-bid auction and Friday Stripe Connect batch can deliver without a second currency. The one harmless use of the word "credit" is as a euro-pegged display label on the principal's prepaid Stripe balance, and even there the agent-benchmark literature gives no reason to expect an AI agent to spend more because the unit has a different name. The recommended default for ADR 0002 is the plain cash wallet, with an optional euro label on the principal side, no platform-held participant balance, and non-convertible participant credits rejected.

## Closed loops hoard credits when earners and spenders are different people

The ten credit economies in the case notes sort into two groups, and the split is decided by one structural fact: whether the people who earn the credit are also the people who want to spend it. Where they are not, the loop seizes. Japan's Fureai Kippu time credits are the best-measured example. In 2010 the Nippon Active Life Club's 12,367 volunteers earned 198,091 credits, but only **10,548 (5%) were redeemed**; against an accumulated stock of 1,678,210 banked hours the redemption rate was **0.6%**, and the organisation only functioned because 55% of service hours were paid for in cash user fees ([Hayashi 2012](https://www.ijccr.net/article/view/9150)). A 1996 survey had already found under 5% of accumulated credits ever redeemed, and the reason given is the asymmetry: user fees generate earning opportunities but "do little to generate matching 'using' opportunities" ([Hayashi 2012](https://www.ijccr.net/article/view/9150)). UK and US time-bank evaluations describe the same shape from the other end: high-skilled members refused to offer coding or legal services, some used the bank "just as a place to give charity, which allows one-sided trades", and brokers rather than members ran the exchanges ([Time Banks in the United Kingdom, 2021](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/93296/1/Time%20Banks%20in%20the%20United%20Kingdom-%20An%20Examination%20of%20the%20Evolution.pdf)). NALC's founder drew the lesson that matters most for A2H: he had "seen many organisations, adopting a money-convertible policy, go bankrupt because of the shortage of pooled cash or confronted with the threat of legal action" ([Hayashi 2012](https://www.ijccr.net/article/view/9150)). A convertible credit is a cash promise, and the issuer must hold the backing cash.

The symmetric-population loops fared no better where the credit could not buy anything outside the loop. Peerage of Science charged authors two review credits per manuscript and paid reviewers one, had Springer Nature and Taylor & Francis as customers, received **102 submissions in 2017** and went out of business soon after ([Wikipedia: Peerage of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peerage_of_Science)). Academic Karma had facilitated **39 reviews** by January 2015, and the critique that stuck was that a currency "has no value until you can use it to buy something" ([Software Sustainability Institute, 2015](https://www.software.ac.uk/blog/currency-peer-review-pubcreds-and-academic-karma)). Lang-8's free-to-earn correction points attracted "spammers and bots", it froze registrations in 2017 and shut on 29 February 2024 ([Wikipedia: Lang-8.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lang-8.com)). Quora launched Credits in November 2011 with 500 free credits per user and a 50-credit cost to ask, dropped the topic-promotion fee within a month, and the feature later disappeared without an announcement ([TechCrunch, Nov 2011](https://techcrunch.com/2011/11/14/quora-gamifies-credits-and-ask-to-answer-suggestions-live-for-everyone)). Brainly's points reward quantity: teachers describe "point farmers" and content "clogging up the system with less-than-helpful content" ([Common Sense Education](https://www.commonsense.org/education/reviews/brainly)).

The exception proves the rule. Stack Overflow bounties work because the same people ask and answer, reputation carries site privileges, and the stock is enormous. A bounty lifts the solve rate of a 100-day-old question from **1.7% to 55%**, but a larger bounty does not make the answer come faster ([Zhou et al., EMSE 2019](https://sailresearch.github.io/sail-website/data/pdfs/EMSE2019_BountiesOnTechnicalQASites_ACaseStudyOfStackOverflowBounties.pdf)), and a second study found "no operationalization of bounty amount shows significance" for quality or latency ([PMC, 2018](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5856353/)). The attention mechanism transfers to A2H; the currency does not, because A2H's experts earn money, not status, and will not accumulate a non-cash unit to spend on asking questions of AI agents.

Two further cases define the political failure modes. Upwork converted free bidding Connects into a purchasable token in 2019; **55% of 463 surveyed freelancers rated the change 1 out of 10** and 45% planned to explore other platforms ([Entrepreneur, 2019](https://www.entrepreneur.com/living/upwork-to-charge-freelancers-to-bid-on-jobs-further/331700)), yet Upwork's 10-K named Connects pricing among its growth drivers ([Upwork 10-K FY2019](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1627475/000162747520000006/upwk-20191231.htm)) and its marketplace take rate reached **18.7% in FY2025** on the back of monetisation products ([Upwork 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1627475/000162747526000012/upwk-20251231.htm)). The later boosted-proposal auction drew "pay-to-win" complaints from freelancers charged for attention that never converted ([Upwork community, Dec 2023](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Freelancers/Proposal-Boosting-System/m-p/1477240)). The lesson is that a credit charged to the supply side is a revenue product that the supply side experiences as a tax; A2H's experts are its supply side. Reddit shows the other failure: it stopped selling Coins on 13 July 2023 with "past purchases are non-refundable" ([TechCrunch, July 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/14/reddit-is-killing-its-gold-awards-system/)), wound down blockchain Community Points in November 2023 because "the resourcing needed was unfortunately too high to justify" plus regulatory burden ([TechCrunch, Oct 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/17/reddit-is-phasing-out-community-points-blockchain-rewards)), and by May 2024 admitted the approach "was the wrong direction" and announced a make-good programme for users who lost coins ([r/reddit, May 2024](https://rl.klein.ruhr/r/reddit/comments/1css0ws/we_heard_you_awards_are_back/l47r6w9)). What Reddit kept is instructive: the Contributor Program pays cash for received gold at **USD 0.90 to 1.00 per gold**, with a 10-gold monthly minimum, identity verification via Persona, Stripe payouts and US-only eligibility ([Android Authority, Sept 2023](https://androidauthority.com/reddit-contributor-program-announced-3368236)). The moment a credit became cash-redeemable, Reddit had to add KYC, a threshold, a cadence and withholding rules, which is exactly the apparatus A2H's cash wallet already has.

The survivors in the corpus are all money-denominated with the platform as counterparty: italki pays teachers in credits at a fixed 10 ITC per USD into a wallet they convert through third-party withdrawal providers ([italki instructor terms](https://www.italki.com/en/instructorterms)), MDPI pays reviewers in APC discount vouchers against its own fees ([MDPI reviewers page](https://www.mdpi.com/reviewers)), and Fenda, the closest structural analogue to A2H, charged askers an expert's self-set cash price, took 10%, refunded unanswered questions after 48 hours, and got **85% of answers within a day** with "a clear cut-off at 48 hours" ([Jan et al., 2017](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.01333)). None of these is a closed loop. They are cash with a label and a payout pipe.

## Cash outperforms points, and experts have never been offered points

The behavioural evidence on payment form is consistent and runs against stated preference. In a 2026 randomised 2x2 experiment on a US panel wave, end-of-field response rates were **68% for a USD 5 Amazon e-gift card, 69% for USD 5 cash, 68% for a split USD 2 + USD 3 gift card, and 79% for split USD 2 + USD 3 cash**; splitting helped only with cash, and the split-cash arm also had the lowest cost per complete and the lowest nonresponse bias ([Survey Research Methods, 2026](https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/srm/article/download/8476/7942/37242?inline=1)). The same respondents, asked what they preferred, chose an Amazon gift card about half the time; behaviour and stated preference diverged ([Survey Research Methods, 2026](https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/srm/article/download/8476/7942/37242?inline=1)). A Swiss panel experiment found a banknote beat a cash card and a pen with the other effects "much lower and statistically insignificant" ([Social Science Research, 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0049089X18305581)). The vendor surveys that find gift cards win are run by gift-card issuers and typically omit bank or PayPal cash as an option ([Blackhawk Network](https://blackhawknetwork.com/solutions/rewards-incentives/customer-loyalty/market-research)); the panel-research survey that included money found **60% preferred money** with Amazon cards a distant second at 25% ([RMS, 2015](https://rmsresults.com/2015/05/19/most-preferred-survey-incentives-by-research-panel-participants/)). The lived experience of restricted value is the sharpest data point: MTurk workers in seven countries paid only in Amazon gift balance said it "cannot cover basic needs", that converting it loses "a significant share" of wages and is "almost a secondary job", and all wanted a bank deposit option ([Data Workers' Inquiry, 2024](https://data-workers.org/?p=508)).

For A2H's first market, the benchmark is not survey panels but expert networks, and every one found pays cash. AlphaSights pays hourly pro-rata on "standard terms of payment are 30 days" ([AlphaSights](https://www.alphasights.com/experts/)); GLG says "most payments to our experts are processed within 7 to 10 business days" ([GLG on Trustpilot](https://at.trustpilot.com/review/glginsights.com?page=7)); Clarity.fm takes 15% and pays via PayPal within 14 days ([Clarity.fm](https://clarity.fm/help/articles/34/how-do-i-get-paid)). None asks a doctor or lawyer to accumulate credits, and the absence of such offers is itself the signal. A Friday Stripe Connect batch is faster than any of these; a credits layer that deferred cash by a further cycle would make A2H the slowest payer in its category. For the second market, members of the public acting as sensors, the benchmark is Prolific: a **GBP 6 / USD 6 minimum**, one cash-out per 24 hours, and a 72-hour cool-off for new or changed payout details ([Prolific participant help](https://participant-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445064-i-m-having-issues-cashing-out)). At €2 to €60 per answer, a €6 to €10 threshold is reached after one or two answers; a €30 to €50 threshold is InboxDollars and YouGov territory, where the dominant complaint is the slow climb to payout ([The Penny Hoarder on YouGov](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/make-money/yougov-review/)).

The complaint surfaces of the two platform families differ in kind, and this is the crux for the "strong incentives" goal. Cash platforms generate identity friction: name and address mismatches, country-mismatch errors, the Hyperwallet migration resetting every Prolific account to the cool-off ([Prolific participant help](https://participant-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445064-i-m-having-issues-cashing-out)). Points platforms generate value loss: an analysis of 2026 Swagbucks reviews found **179 describing a payout that never completed, 73 of which say the account was shut with a balance inside** ([unstar.app, 2026](https://unstar.app/blog/does-swagbucks-actually-pay-account-deactivated-cash-out-reviews-2026)); Google Opinion Rewards began enforcing one-year expiry in November 2019 and wiped balances, prompting a wave of complaints ([Android Police, 2019](https://www.androidpolice.com/2019/11/13/google-opinion-rewards-credits-disappearing-update/)). A2H's Friday batch plus identity verification lagging until payout already sits on the cash-platform side of that line. A credits layer would import the second complaint class without removing the first.

The breakage economics explain why points platforms behave as they do, and why A2H should not want that revenue. The only hard panel-incentive breakage data in the corpus is Greenfield Online's SEC correspondence: forfeitures ran **7 to 13% of gross incentive expense in North America**, where panelists were notified and given 30 days, and **25 to 48% in Europe**, where they were not ([Greenfield Online SEC correspondence, 2008](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001108906/000095012308006525/filename1.htm)). Silent expiry roughly tripled breakage, and under IFRS 15 and ASC 606 expected breakage is recognised as revenue in proportion to redemption ([PwC Revenue guide](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/pwc/accounting_guides/revenue_from_contrac/revenue_from_contrac_US/chapter_7_options_to_US/74unexercised_rights_US.html)), which is precisely the accounting pull that turns a credits ledger into a points programme over time. Loyalty points are a marketing cost the issuer wants partly unredeemed; unredeemed expert earnings on A2H would be a trust liability.

What rewards do move is speed, and only when paired with a deadline. Yahoo Answers questions with a point offer got a best answer about **five times faster** but attracted fewer answers ([Choi, Kitzie & Shah, 2013](https://scholarcommons.sc.edu/libsci_facpub/223)); Google Answers' higher cash price bought "a significantly longer, but not better, answer" ([Chen, Ho & Kim, 2008](https://mail.yanchen.people.si.umich.edu/papers/google_answer_20081124.pdf)); Fenda's 48-hour refund cut-off, not its price level, did the work on latency ([Jan et al., 2017](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.01333)). On quality, higher pay on Prolific reduced attrition and correlated with more accurate performance in a 225-participant randomised study ([Ritchey et al., PLOS ONE 2023](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10550147/)), but the large platform gaps are explained by vetting, not payment form: at identical pay, Prolific produced **67.9% high-quality respondents against MTurk's 26.4%**, with the authors crediting closed pools and identity checks ([Douglas, Ewell & Brauer, PLOS ONE 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10013894/)); a 2023 remote-trial recruitment found **75.6% of early enrolees were bots** until a CAPTCHA was added ([JMIR, 2023](https://www.jmir.org/2023//e46523/)). Fraud follows liquidity plus weak identity, not the name of the unit. Credits redeemable for cash do not deter farms unless redemption is gated on verification, in which case the gate does the work, and A2H's "verify before first payout" on a cash wallet already provides it.

## A platform-issued convertible credit is e-money in France

The regulatory analysis is where the credit idea becomes expensive rather than merely unhelpful. EMD2 defines electronic money as electronically stored monetary value, issued on receipt of funds for the purpose of making payment transactions, and accepted by persons other than the issuer ([DNB](https://www.dnb.nl/en/sector-information/supervision-sectors/electronic-money-institutions/authorisation-as-an-electronic-money-institution-overview/what-is-an-electronic-money-institution/)). A credit that a principal buys for cash, that is used to pay a participant (a person other than the issuer) and that the participant can redeem for cash satisfies every limb. Issuing e-money without authorisation is an offence; in France the equivalent is agrément by the ACPR under the Code monétaire et financier. PSD2 and EMD2 remain the law in force: PSD3 and the PSR reached provisional agreement on 27 November 2025, but as of early September 2026 both files were still awaiting the Council's first-reading position with an indicative plenary date of 14 December 2026, pushing entry into force into 2027 and main application toward mid-2028 ([ClearingPost, Sept 2026](https://clearingpost.com/insights/legislative-observatory-lists-december-14-indicative-plenary-date-for-psd3-and-p/)).

The two exclusions a marketplace could invoke are narrow and narrowing. The limited-network exclusion requires a direct contractual agreement with each acceptor, a stated maximum number of providers and a common brand ([EBA/GL/2022/02](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/contentassets/fe9063c85d0a4a48bd8a155055c99c43/2022_2_en_final-report.pdf)); A2H's acceptors would be an open, growing pool of individual participants, and the EBA's own feedback table says online marketplaces "tend to continuously grow their acceptance network" so authorities "should treat these business models with caution" ([EBA/GL/2022/02](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/contentassets/fe9063c85d0a4a48bd8a155055c99c43/2022_2_en_final-report.pdf)). Guideline 1.9 adds that redeemability is simply ignored for the exclusion test, so being refundable neither helps nor hurts, and notification is due once transactions exceed **EUR 1 million** in any period under 12 months. The commercial-agent exclusion applies only where the platform acts for the payer or the payee, not both; the EBA's answer is that "PSD2 does not provide criteria in order to determine whether a platform can be deemed to be also acting on behalf of the payer" and the competent authority must judge ([EBA Q&A 2020_5355](https://eba.europa.eu/single-rule-book-qa/qna/view/publicId/2020_5355)). The ACPR is reported to have said the exemption "ne s'applique pas aux places de marché telles qu'elles fonctionnent actuellement" ([Actu-Juridique](https://www.actu-juridique.fr/affaires/bancaire-credit/precisions-de-lacpr-a-propos-de-situations-entrainant-des-exemptions-dagrement/)), a quote taken from a search index because the page returned 403 and therefore to be verified before it is relied on. The PSR retains both exclusions but narrows the agent one, with commentators expecting marketplaces' reliance to shrink ([Simmons & Simmons](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/cmanzeyn1009cvch0q9u61uva/relevant-exclusions-under-the-psd3-psr)).

Stripe's position closes the loop. Connect platforms do not need a licence because end-customer funds "are never in the platform's possession or control" and sit in Stripe-controlled safeguarded accounts; with separate charges and transfers the platform "provides pre-agreed instructions to Stripe" and "has no discretionary authority over the funds", and French Connect platforms "must use account tokens" to reduce the risk of being seen as an intermediary ([Stripe Connect & PSD2 FAQ](https://stripe.com/sv-fi/guides/frequently-asked-questions-about-stripe-connect-and-psd2)). But Stripe's Restricted Businesses list, updated 22 September 2026, restricts "sale of stored value or credits maintained, accepted and issued by anyone other than the seller" and warns that "seller-maintained stored value or credits may be subject to limits", alongside restricted categories for "virtual credits or other products and services in which a monetary value is stored within the item" and "payment facilitation and aggregation (including receiving settlement proceeds for goods or services that you did not provide)" ([Stripe Restricted Businesses](https://stripe.com/en-gb/legal/restricted-businesses)). Stripe Billing's "credits" are a usage-pricing feature with no documented cash-out or transfer path to connected accounts ([Stripe docs: billing credits](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/usage-based/billing-credits)). A platform-issued cash-redeemable credit is at best a restricted use case needing Stripe's approval; the plain Connect model is the structure Stripe has designed its licence around.

If the credit is e-money, French law gives the holder rights A2H cannot design around on the participant side. CMF Articles L.133-29 to L.133-38 provide redemption at par on request, "sans frais pour le détenteur", with transfer costs for cash refunds falling on the issuer notwithstanding any clause; Article L.133-37 allows contractual derogation only where the holder is not "une personne physique agissant à des fins non professionnelles" ([Légifrance CMF L.133-29 à L.133-38](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072026/LEGISCTA000027005748/); [Art. L.133-37](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000027010016)). Business principals can be made to waive refund rights by contract; individual participants cannot, so expiry or forfeiture of earned convertible balances would be the highest-risk consumer-law feature of any credit system. On AML, the only simplified due diligence available to e-money under the EU AMLR applying from 10 July 2027 requires an instrument that is not reloadable, holds at most EUR 150, is "used exclusively to purchase goods or services provided by the issuer" and "does not permit any stored amount to be exchanged for cash" ([AMLR Art. 19](https://www.springlex.eu/en/packages/aml/amlr-regulation/article-19/); [Didit AMLR checklist](https://didit.me/de/blog/amlr-checklist-by-obliged-entity/)). A convertible credit fails that test on its face, so A2H as issuer would run full customer due diligence on every principal and participant, transaction monitoring and reporting to TRACFIN, whereas under Connect the regulated KYC on connected accounts is Stripe's obligation.

The accounting and tax angles give nothing back. The IFRS Interpretations Committee's analysis of a prepaid card redeemable at third parties concluded the issuer has "a contractual obligation to deliver cash" and the balance is a financial liability under IAS 32/IFRS 9, with breakage following derecognition rules rather than IFRS 15 ([IAS Plus, Sept 2015](https://www.iasplus.com/en/meeting-notes/ifrs-ic/2015/september/ias-32); [IFRS staff paper, Jan 2016](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/meetings/2016/january/ifrs-ic/ias-32-classification-of-liability/ap5-prepaid-card-and-comment-letters.pdf)). Participant-earned convertible credits would therefore be a financial liability of A2H from the moment they are credited, a balance-sheet item that does not exist when each accepted answer triggers a Stripe transfer. DAC7 defines reportable consideration as compensation "paid or credited" to the seller net of platform fees, with no de minimis for services ([Belastingdienst DAC7 guidance](https://belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/en/business/content/information-for-platform-operators-dac7)); France transposed it into CGI Articles 1649 ter A to E with first reports due by 31 January 2024 ([Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/tax/france-electronic-platforms-the-dac-7-directive-is-transposed-with-an-effective-date-of-1-january-2024-for-transactions-carried-out-in-2023)). Crediting a balance is already the reportable event, so credits defer nothing and add a valuation step. On VAT, the voucher directive targets "only vouchers which can be used for redemption against goods or services" ([Directive 2016/1065](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2016/1065/data.htm?view=plain)), so a cash-redeemable credit looks like a payment instrument rather than a voucher; if it were a voucher it would be multi-purpose, with VAT at redemption, and Article 30b(2) taxes "any supply of services that can be identified" by an intermediary, which means a spread hidden in the credit price is taxed exactly like an explicit commission ([CJEU C-68/23 via Meijburg](https://meijburg.com/news/cjeu-rules-vat-treatment-vouchers)). The lightest compliant design is the one already planned: principals prepay into Stripe, participants are Stripe Express or Custom connected accounts, each accepted answer triggers a transfer, and any "credit" is a display label with a fixed euro value, no independent redemption promise by A2H, and no platform-held earned balance.

## Principal-side prepaid credits are a budgeting wallet, not a growth lever

The buyer side is the half of the proposal that is low-risk, and also the half where the evidence for an incentive effect is weakest. Every usage platform examined sells prepaid balances 1:1 to invoice currency, expiring after about a year, non-refundable and with no bundle discount. OpenAI's stated reason is budgeting: prepaid billing "helps developers know what they are committing to upfront" ([OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8264644-what-is-prepaid-billing)). Anthropic's credit terms state credits "expire one calendar year" from confirmation, are "non-refundable", cannot be transferred or sold, and "are not legal tender or currency of any kind and have no cash value" ([Anthropic credit terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/credit-terms)), language whose purpose is to stay outside e-money rules and block secondary markets. Twilio reserves postpaid invoicing for customers with "a committed annual spend of at least $12,000 USD" after a credit check ([Twilio](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/223135647-Can-I-set-up-billing-by-invoice-for-my-Twilio-project-)). The closest analogue to A2H is Prolific: researchers top up a workspace balance by card or bank transfer, receive an invoice per top-up, and pay a platform fee of **42.8% for corporate customers or 33.3% for academia** calculated on top of participant rewards ([Prolific pricing](https://researcher-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445239-what-is-your-pricing)). The economic functions of these balances are credit-risk elimination for small unverified accounts, a gating ladder that unlocks limits as cumulative spend proves the account, and predictability. None is an incentive to sign up; all are reasons the platform prefers prepayment.

The "credits make people spend more" argument rests on consumer mental-accounting research that does not reach A2H's buyer. The denomination effect shows people spend small units more readily than one large note ([Raghubir & Srivastava, 2009](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/jconrs/v36y2009i4p701-713.html)); a 2026 randomised trial with 753 UK participants found willingness to pay for loot boxes "significantly higher when transactions used virtual currencies with a 1:1 exchange rate compared to British pounds", and non-intuitive exchange rates added nothing further ([Toccafondi, Di Paolo & Di Guida, 2026](https://iris.imtlucca.it/handle/20.500.11771/42198)); a competing laboratory study found results that "don't support the notion that the currency conversions would lead to increased spending" ([Aalto](https://research.aalto.fi/fi/publications/economic-decision-making-in-free-to-play-games-a-laboratory-exper/)). All of this was measured on individuals with small stakes; nothing tests corporate procurement. At A2H the bid is placed by an agent against a numeric per-request cap and monthly budget, which is the worst possible audience for a framing effect. The agent benchmarks point the other way: frontier agents are "overly optimistic about remaining budget", with budget awareness correlating only about 0.35 with task skill ([BAGEN](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.00198)); agents told a budget is "around N" under-spend far more often than they over-spend ([ComboShoppingBench](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.09282)); and every model tested sent part of its procurement budget to fraudulent suppliers ([E-Commerce Bench](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.30730)). Any uplift from a credit label would have to act on the human principal setting the budget, and even that is untested in B2B.

Signup and referral credits are the one principal-side mechanism with a plausible acquisition effect, and they carry a specific fraud surface that A2H's two-sided design makes worse. The canonical results (Dropbox, PayPal) are pre-2010 marketing lore without cost-per-acquired-user figures; the documented failure is Uber's 2015 case of a student who earned about **USD 50,000 in self-promoted referral credits** that Uber cancelled ([Buyapowa](https://www.buyapowa.com/blog/referral-programs-fraud-gaming.md)). The guardrails platforms actually published are to pay only on a verified qualifying action, delay rewards past the refund window, cap them, and reserve clawback; Coinbase pays referral bonuses only after a qualifying purchase within a set window and only for brand-new accounts ([Coinbase referral program](https://help.coinbase.com/coinbase/getting-started/getting-started-with-coinbase/new-customer-incentive/simple-referral)). For A2H the loop closes whenever one actor controls both a principal account holding free credits and a participant account receiving Friday payouts: post requests, answer them, convert bonus credit into cash net of the fee. Discounted bundles create the same arbitrage at the discount margin. Industrialised airdrop farming "became the dominant manifestation of Sybil activity between 2023 and 2025", and rule-based clustering "can generate unacceptable false-positive rates" ([MDPI Applied Sciences, 2025](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/14/6929)), so detection must be probabilistic with manual review. The transferable defences are to issue bonus credit only to a verified business account, bar bonus-funded requests from being answered by any participant sharing device, IP, bank account or Connect identity with the principal, pay referral rewards only after the referee's first captured payment from their own card, keep bonus credit non-transferable and expiring, and hold the participant-side payout for bonus-funded answers through a review window.

Two design points about the auction and the fee follow from the same evidence. Denominating reserve, bid and clearing price in a floating proprietary unit buys nothing: Upwork's boosted-proposal auction, the one live example of a mini-auction in a non-1:1 unit, generates sustained complaints from freelancers "getting charged without getting hired" ([Upwork community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Support-Forum/Marketplace-Updates-Boosted-Proposals-and-Connects/m-p/1271430)), Reddit's floating tokens fell **84%** on the day the programme was cancelled ([Coin360](https://coin360.com/news/Reddit-Community-Points-Program-Terminated)), and the 2026 RCT shows a 1:1 unit already captures whatever money-illusion uplift exists. A 1:1 euro unit keeps bid, hold and capture in the same number the agent's cap uses, and any paid influence on ordering must in any case be disclosed in plain language under P2B Article 5, which Italy's AGCOM enforced against Refurbed in November 2025 ([BDK Advokati](https://bdkadvokati.com/p2b-regulation-has-teeth-french-and-italian-regulators-show); [Mason Hayes & Curran](https://mhc.ie/latest/insights/the-p2b-regulation)). On the fee, none of the comparable platforms hides the take inside a credit price: MTurk charged an explicit **20%** on top of the worker reward ([MTurk pricing](https://www.mturk.com/pricing)) and Prolific an explicit percentage on top ([Prolific pricing](https://researcher-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445239-what-is-your-pricing)); Stripe's `application_fee_amount` is "the only fee that your connected accounts see" and is capped at the captured amount ([Stripe: application fees](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/application-fees)). Gurley's warning that "the most dangerous strategy for any platform company is to price too high" is about level, not placement ([Above the Crowd, 2013](https://abovethecrowd.com/2013/04/18/a-rake-too-far/)); placement decides who sees the number, and the Upwork record suggests a participant-side fee invites the perception that the platform takes from earnings, which an ADR on money model should weigh explicitly.

## Four options for ADR 0002, with the evidence for and against each

The table below sets the four options the ADR must at least consider against the criteria that the evidence shows matter: incentive effect on signup and participation, regulatory weight in France, fraud surface, operational and accounting complexity, and the trust risk of value loss.

| Option | What it is | Evidence for | Evidence against | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Plain cash wallet as planned | Principals fund a Stripe balance; hold = bid; participants paid cleared price minus fee via Stripe Connect on Fridays | Cash beat gift cards 79% vs 68% behaviourally; all expert networks pay cash at net 7 to 30 days, so Friday beats the category; Prolific's cash model with identity gating yields the highest data quality; Stripe Connect keeps A2H outside PSD2/EMD2 licensing and makes KYC Stripe's regulated duty; no financial liability, no breakage question, no expiry problem | Identity friction at first payout is the complaint surface (name/address mismatch, cool-offs); no "feel-free" spend effect for principals, though none is evidenced for agents anyway; card authorisation per request has a 7-day expiry constraint unless prepayment is used | Recommended default |
| B. Euro-denominated "credits" as a label over Stripe-held funds | Same as A; the principal's prepaid balance is displayed as credits at a fixed 1 credit = €1, with no redemption promise by A2H and no participant-side balance | Matches the OpenAI/Anthropic/Prolific prepaid-balance pattern; a 1:1 unit captures whatever framing uplift exists (2026 RCT); gives a natural home for promotional/bonus credit with non-transferable, expiring, no-cash-value terms; keeps bid, hold and capture in one unit; DAC7 valuation trivial | Adds no measurable incentive for agent buyers; the word invites scope creep toward platform-issued value (EBA Guideline 1.3 means the Stripe top-up does not sanctify a platform instrument); bonus credits open the principal/participant arbitrage loop unless gated; Stripe's "seller-maintained credits may be subject to limits" needs confirming | Acceptable as a presentation layer on A only, with hard invariants |
| C. True platform-issued convertible credit | A2H issues credits bought by principals and earned by participants, redeemable for cash on A2H's promise | None in the corpus: no two-sided convertible credit economy for a B2B or agent-to-human marketplace was found; the cases that converted (Reddit Contributor Program, italki) are cash payouts with a label | E-money in substance (EMD2 definition met); LNE and commercial-agent exclusions weak and narrowing; ACPR agrément or agent arrangement; CMF L.133-29ff refund at par, free, unwaivable for individuals; full LCB-FT obliged-entity programme and AMLR Art. 19(7) exemption unavailable; financial liability under IAS 32 with no breakage revenue; Stripe restricted category; no VAT or DAC7 advantage; cash-convertible promises without backing cash bankrupted time banks (Takahata) | Reject |
| D. Non-convertible participant credits | Participants earn credits spendable only on A2H (asking, boosting, badges), not cashable | Stack Overflow bounties show an attention currency can work where the same people earn and spend and the unit carries status | A2H's experts are earners and principals are spenders, the Fureai Kippu asymmetry (0.6% redemption); "no value until you can buy something" (Academic Karma, Quora); free-to-earn credit invites bots (Lang-8, Brainly farmers); experts have never been offered points by any network; restricted value experienced as a wage cut (MTurk gift balance); breakage would be a trust liability and accounting would push it into revenue | Reject for payment; a non-monetary reputation score is a separate, later question |

The recommended default is Option A with Option B's label permitted only under invariants. The reasoning is that the goal, strong incentives to sign up and participate, is best served by the properties the cash wallet already has and by four additions that cost nothing in regulatory weight: a payout threshold of **no more than about €10** so that one or two answers reach it; a published Friday cadence with a visible pending balance (Prolific's "approved balance" pattern); an explicit, machine-legible percentage fee rather than a spread; and identity verification that gates the first payout, which is the control the Prolific/MTurk quality gap actually attributes results to. The split-cash finding suggests a fifth lever that the reserve-plus-bid auction supports natively: showing participants a guaranteed reserve now plus a conditional bid uplift on acceptance mirrors the USD 2 + USD 3 structure that out-performed every other arm ([Survey Research Methods, 2026](https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/srm/article/download/8476/7942/37242?inline=1)). Fenda's 48-hour refund cut-off suggests a sixth: a visible deadline after which the hold is released concentrates answers in the first day ([Jan et al., 2017](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.01333)). None of these requires a second currency, and each is undermined by one.

## What to write into the ADR and what remains open

The decision statement should say that A2H's money model is euros held by Stripe at every point in the lifecycle: principal prepayment into a Stripe customer balance or manual-capture PaymentIntent, hold equal to bid, capture on acceptance, platform fee taken as a Stripe application fee, and participant payout by transfer to a Stripe Express or Custom connected account in the Friday batch. The append-only ledger records these events in euros and mirrors Stripe; it is never the source of truth for a balance that is a claim against A2H. Where the word "credit" appears in product copy, the ADR should fix the invariants that keep it a label: 1 credit = €1 and never floats; a credit exists only as a display of funds Stripe already holds for that principal; A2H makes no independent redemption promise; there is no participant-side credit balance, only the pending-then-paid Stripe transfer; earned value never expires; principal prepayments carry a stated refund-on-retirement rule so that an Option B label can never produce a Reddit-style confiscation; promotional credit is non-transferable, expiring, no-cash-value and issued only to a business account with a verified payment method, following the Anthropic terms, and requests funded by it cannot be answered by a participant linked to the principal by device, IP, bank account or Connect identity. The ADR should record the four rejected or constrained options and the one-line reason for each: C because it is e-money in France with no offsetting benefit; D because A2H's populations are asymmetric and experts are paid in money; B's floating or discounted variants because they create arbitrage and opacity. It should also note the triggers for revisiting: if A2H ever needs principal balances outside Stripe, that is an EMI question and not a product tweak; if the public-sensor market ever grows a genuine spend side, Option D can be re-examined against Stack Overflow's conditions.

Several items are open and belong in the ADR as explicit unknowns rather than silent assumptions. Stripe's limits on "seller-maintained stored value or credits" are not elaborated publicly and must be asked of Stripe before any Option B label ships ([Stripe Restricted Businesses](https://stripe.com/en-gb/legal/restricted-businesses)). The choice between per-request manual-capture holds and a prepaid Stripe customer balance is a payments-engineering decision the notes do not settle; the prepaid route avoids one authorisation per request and the authorisation-expiry problem, which is the only genuine operational argument for a balance at all. Fee placement (participant side as planned, or on top on the principal side as MTurk and Prolific do) is not forced by any of the evidence but changes what each side sees and how the agent's cap is expressed, and it should be decided in the same ADR. The ACPR's reported statement on marketplaces and the commercial-agent exemption must be verified against the ACPR text, since the quote came from a search index. The French income-tax timing for a participant paid in convertible value is unsourced; the design should not rely on any deferral, and the proposed platform withholding of micro-entrepreneurs' social contributions, piloted from 1 October 2026 and planned for generalisation on 1 January 2027 but "pas encore voté", may add a withholding step to the Friday batch ([jechange.fr](https://www.jechange.fr/banques/news/micro-entrepreneurs-plateformes-precompte-cotisations-2027-200-millions)). The final PSR text and its application date (18 versus 21 months after entry into force) are unsettled. B2B unfair-terms treatment of non-refundable principal prepayments under Code de commerce L.442-1 was not researched. And no platform in the corpus has published an A/B result for signup credits versus cash, so any acquisition effect A2H expects from promotional credit is a hypothesis to test, not a finding to build on.

## Conclusion

The question was whether a credit system would create stronger incentives to sign up and participate than a cash wallet. The research reframes it: the incentive properties A2H wants are properties of cash delivered quickly, reliably and in full, and every documented credit system either converged on exactly that (italki, Fenda, Reddit's Contributor Program) or failed by moving away from it. The structural reason is that A2H has the one population shape under which earn-to-spend loops cannot close, experts who only earn and agents who only spend, and the regulatory reason is that in France the moment A2H itself promises to turn a unit into euros it has issued electronic money, with everything that entails and nothing in return.

What the evidence does unlock is a set of cheap, cash-native design moves that the reserve-plus-bid auction happens to fit unusually well: a visible guaranteed reserve plus a conditional uplift, a low threshold, a hard deadline on the hold, an explicit fee, and verification at first payout rather than at signup. Those are the levers worth A/B testing in the expert phase before the public-sensor phase, and they leave the ledger, Stripe and the ADR simpler rather than more complex. The word "credit" can survive as a label on the principal's prepaid euros if the invariants above are written down; the thing itself should not.
